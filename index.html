नागरिक सहायता Nagrik Sahayata

अपने अधिकार जानिए, सही कदम उठाइए — हिंदी और English में।

Step-by-step guides for RTI, consumer complaints, police complaints, cybercrime reporting and government grievances — plus ready-to-use letter templates and a community help board. Free. No sign-up. Your data stays in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

गाइड देखें ↓ पत्र बनाएँ ✉️

🆘 आपातकाल Emergency: 112  ·  ऑनलाइन ठगी Online fraud: 1930  ·  उपभोक्ता Consumer helpline: 1915

📖 अधिकार गाइड — Know your rights

Search in Hindi or English. Tick off each step as you complete it — your progress is saved in this browser.

✉️ पत्र लेखक — Letter writer

Fill the form, generate a formal Hindi/English letter, then copy it or download it as a .txt file. Nothing is sent anywhere — the letter is generated on your device.

🤝 मदद बोर्ड — Community help board

Ask for help or offer it — a neighbour who knows the RTI process, someone to accompany you to the police station, a translator for a form. Posts are stored only in this browser (localStorage). Share no OTPs, passwords, bank details or full ID numbers here.

ℹ️ जानकारी — About & limits

  • What it is: a free, offline-first guide + letter-writing tool. It explains procedures and drafts letters — it is not legal advice and it does not file anything for you. Always check the official portal linked in each guide before acting.
  • Your data: checklists and board posts live only in your browser's localStorage. Clearing browser data erases them. No account, no server, no tracking.
  • Official sources linked: rtionline.gov.in (RTI), consumerhelpline.gov.in + edaakhil.nic.in (consumer), cybercrime.gov.in + helpline 1930 (cybercrime), pgportal.gov.in (CPGRAMS), nfsa.gov.in (ration). Fees and timelines (e.g. RTI ₹10, 30-day reply) follow the RTI Act, 2005 and the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 as published on those portals.
  • Built by: Mohd Raja (devilking7x) for Code for Change 2026. Source: github.com/devilking7x/nagrik-sahayata.
